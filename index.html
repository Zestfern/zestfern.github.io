How we make our books

Small studio, careful process. Every book goes through the same four steps before it reaches you.

  1. Choose a themeWe start with topics people genuinely enjoy – seasons, hobbies, holidays and favorite animals.
  2. Design for easy useClear fonts, generous margins and one puzzle per page. Thicker lines and simpler shapes in our kids' books.
  3. Check every puzzleEach sudoku is tested to have exactly one solution, every maze to have a way out and every hidden word to be findable. Then each book is reviewed page by page.
  4. Printed by AmazonAmazon prints every copy on demand and ships it to you, with Amazon's usual delivery and returns.

About Zestfern

Zestfern is a small, independent publishing studio. We make puzzle books, coloring books and journals that are simply pleasant to use – for grandparents who want bigger print, for kids who are just picking up a pencil, and for anyone who enjoys a quiet moment with a good puzzle.

We keep things simple: we design and check the books, and Amazon prints and delivers them. That's why you'll find all our titles on Amazon, and nowhere else.

Questions & answers

Where can I buy Zestfern books?

Only on Amazon. Each book page here links directly to the book on Amazon, where you can see the current price and delivery options. We don't sell books directly on this website.

Who prints and ships the books?

Amazon prints each copy on demand and ships it to you. Orders, delivery and returns follow Amazon's standard policies.

Are the answers included?

Yes. Every word search, sudoku, maze, crossword and quiz book has full solutions at the back.

What does "large print" mean in your books?

Big, bold letters in an extra-large grid, with one puzzle per 8.5 × 11 inch page – comfortable to read without squinting.

Does this website collect any data?

No. There are no sign-ups, no forms and no tracking cookies. The site simply shows our books and links to Amazon.
